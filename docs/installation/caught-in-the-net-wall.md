# Caught in the Net: Wall Installation Plan

Cybersecurity Awareness Month experience wall. Theme: "Caught in the Net" (fishing).
Three iPad kiosks running the Caught in the Net arcade (already built, fully offline).
Zeke, the one-eyed navy mascot, stars in the wall art. Budget target: $30,000.

## Design language

Matches the games exactly ("Bait Shop Deco"): vintage tackle-shop meets WPA poster,
underwater. Deep ink teal (#041B26 to #072A3C), warm cream lettering (#F4EBD9),
lure orange (#FF7A45), bobber red (#E8564A), brass gold (#F5C15C), one restrained
bioluminescent cyan (#53DFE8) for glow. Chunky Futura-style caps. Rope and net as
the recurring motif. Zeke is repainted into this world (fishing rod, diving helmet,
tangled in a golden net) rather than pasted on top of it.

## Four concepts (mockups in assets/wall-mockups/)

1. **Bait Shop Storefront** (concept-a): the wall is a tackle-shop facade with a
   marquee sign; Zeke leans out the shop window fishing with a padlock as bait.
   Kiosks are bait-crate podiums. Coziest; strongest photo moment at the window.
2. **Abyss Hero Mural** (concept-b): one giant cinematic underwater mural, Zeke
   caught in a glowing golden net at center, brass porthole kiosks. Boldest single
   image; best for a long sight line across a lobby.
3. **Boardwalk Pier** (concept-c): dusk pier scene, Zeke reeling in a phishing
   envelope-fish, dock-piling kiosks, printed water floor decal. Most immersive
   floor-to-wall scene; best if you can claim floor space.
4. **Poster Gallery Triptych** (concept-d): three rope-framed WPA posters, one per
   headline game (Reel 'Em In, Kraken Attack, Packet Trawler), each above its own
   kiosk. Cleanest wayfinding: the poster above each iPad advertises the game on it.

All four share one production spec below, so the budget holds for any pick.

## Production spec

- **Backdrop**: 20 ft W x 8 ft H seamless dye-sub tension fabric on aluminum frame
  (SEG). Print file at 120 dpi minimum at full size. We can upscale the chosen
  key art to print resolution with the AI upscaler, then a print vendor does
  color-proofing. Wrinkle-free, machine-washable, reusable next year.
- **iPad kiosks (x3)**: floor-standing locking enclosures (Bouncepad Lounge /
  CTA Digital / ArmorActive class), home button hidden, Guided Access on. Each
  kiosk skinned to match its concept (crate wrap, rope wrap, or porthole collar).
  Games run fully offline from the device; localStorage leaderboards per iPad.
- **Zeke dimensional prop**: one 4 to 5 ft carved EPS foam + hardcoat Zeke,
  tangled in a real net (or peeking from behind a kiosk). This is the selfie
  magnet and the budget's hero line item.
- **Props layer**: real knotted fishing net swags, striped buoys, life ring,
  manila rope coils, lobster traps (prop tables for handouts), wooden barrel,
  lanterns with warm LED candles.
- **Lighting**: two blue LED wash bars up-lighting the mural + one water-ripple
  effect light (gobo) sweeping the floor + warm string lights on the facade.
- **Optional**: 43 in TV in a rope frame cycling the Hall of Fame leaderboard
  page (leaderboard.html) so passersby see high scores.

## Sourcing notes

- Backdrop + floor vinyl: any trade-show print house (Freeman, Skyline, or a
  local SEG shop). Ask for SEG fabric, 8 ft frame, rental or purchase.
- Kiosks: Bouncepad, CTA Digital, Displays2Go (locking iPad floor stands).
- Foam Zeke: theme-prop fabricators (FoamCorePro, Dino Rentos, local scenic
  shops quote from a 3D file or turnaround drawings).
- Nautical props: Nautical Seasons, Amazon commercial fishing net suppliers,
  restaurant-decor wholesalers; lobster traps often cheapest from marine
  supply or Facebook Marketplace.
- Lighting: any AV rental house (wash bars + water gobo are stock items).

  golden net, quiet zones left mid-wall for porthole kiosks.
- `banner-c-pier-print.jpg` (3168x1344): dusk pier, Zeke reeling an
  envelope-fish, hanging sign.
- `poster-d1-reel-em-in-print.jpg`, `poster-d2-kraken-attack-print.jpg`,
  `poster-d3-packet-trawler-print.jpg` (1696x2528 each): the WPA poster
  triptych for concept D.

Full-resolution 4x masters (6336x2688 banners, 3392x5056 posters) are saved in
the Magnific account library (same titles); download from there for the print
vendor. Banners were generated at 21:9; the vendor crops or extends the
painted edges to the exact 20x8 ft (5:2) frame. Vendor does final color proof.
Kiosk wraps and the floor decal are cropped from the same art so everything
matches.
